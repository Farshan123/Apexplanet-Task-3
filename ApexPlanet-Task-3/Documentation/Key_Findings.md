## Top 6 insights
1. **34% of orders create 66.5% of revenue** (High + Premium tiers). The Low tier (33% of orders) adds only 7.3%.
2. **Electronics is the engine:** 36.4% of revenue, and 41.4% of Premium-tier revenue. Laptops have the highest Premium order rate (13.5% vs 10% average).
3. **Q3 slump:** revenue falls ~16% below the other quarters (Aug ₹9.45M, Sep ₹9.18M), then rebounds +36.2% in October.
4. **Basket size varies by city, not category:** Bengaluru AOV ₹153.9K vs Gaya ₹122.9K and Delhi ₹128.8K. Pune has the fewest orders (99) but strong baskets.
5. **Customers rarely return:** 94.5% bought once. Retention is the biggest growth lever.
6. **December volume ≠ value:** most orders (98) but low AOV (₹125.5K).

## Recommended actions
1. Focus promotions on Laptops/Mobiles and "upgrade to Premium" offers.
2. Launch a Q3 (Aug–Sep) campaign.
3. Bundles for Gaya and Delhi; reach campaigns for Pune.
4. Introduce a loyalty / repeat-purchase programme.
5. Improve data capture (unique Order_IDs, one ID per customer, mandatory City/Age).

## Data caveats
13 rows lack City, 20 lack Age, Order_ID ORD100050 repeats 9 times, 52 Customer_IDs have conflicting names, and January 2026 is a single day. All are documented and handled in the cleaned dataset (see `Deep_Dive_Analysis.md`).
